Support

Sentinel Arcade contains two games, Sentinel Swarm and Rubble Run. It is free to play.

Contact

Email sentinelarcade@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version, the game you were playing, and a description of the problem.

Tokens

A token buys a continue at the end of a run. New players start with 3 tokens. Each day, if you have fewer than 3, your tokens are refilled to 3. Eleven of the achievements give 1 token each, once.

You can watch an ad to get a token at any time: tap the coin on the arcade floor, then tap Watch an ad for a token. If you have no tokens at the end of a run, you can watch an ad to continue.

Ads

The app shows an ad between games, unless you own Unlimited Mode. Once you have played 3 games and 3 minutes since the last ad, an ad plays before your next game starts. An ad never plays during a game. Watching an ad for a token or to continue counts as your last ad.

Unlimited Mode

Unlimited Mode is a one-time purchase ($1.99 in the United States). It makes continues free and removes ads. It does not unlock the fighters or characters earned through achievements.

Restoring Unlimited Mode

To restore Unlimited Mode on a new phone or after reinstalling the app, tap the gear on the arcade floor to open Settings, then tap Restore purchases. You must be signed in with the Apple Account used to buy it.

Continued runs

When you continue a run, your score resets to 0. A continued run does not count toward your best score, the leaderboards or achievements.

Leaderboards and achievements

Leaderboards and achievements use Apple's Game Center. To sign in, open Settings on your iPhone and tap Game Center. If you reinstall the app and sign in to the same Game Center account, the fighters and characters you earned are restored.

Deleting the app

Best scores, tokens and settings are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app removes them unless you restore your phone from a backup. Unlimited Mode and your Game Center records are not affected.
